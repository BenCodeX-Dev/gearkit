Gearkit

A datapack for Minecraft. One command puts 29 shulker boxes of fully enchanted gear, potions, books and supplies in front of you. Pick a box to look inside.

In your ender chest

Six boxes, in slots 0 to 5.

    On the ground

    23 boxes, 15 blocks in front of you, five to a row.

      Get it

      Download the datapack from Modrinth. The generator and its output are on GitHub, under the MIT licence.

      Take gearkit-1.0.0 for Minecraft 1.21.5 and later, and gearkit-1.0.0-legacy for 1.21 to 1.21.4. I tested them on plain servers running 1.21, 1.21.11 and 26.3.

      Run it

      Put the zip into the datapacks folder of your world. You need cheats switched on, or operator rights on a server.

      /reload
/function gearkit:all

      /function gearkit:give_all puts all 29 boxes into your inventory instead. It has to be almost empty for that.

      The rules

      Every enchantment is at its vanilla maximum and never above it. Fire Aspect and Flame are left out.

      Enchantments that cannot share an item are split over two: a bow with Infinity and one with Mending, a pickaxe with Fortune and one with Silk Touch, and so on.

      The lists on this page are written by a script from the same definitions that build the datapack. Not an official Minecraft product. Not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
